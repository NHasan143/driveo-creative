## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

Driveo Exclusive is a private chauffeur marketing and booking-request website. Visitors explore services, vehicles and operating locations, then request a personally prepared fixed quote.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The existing application offers airport transfers, point-to-point journeys, hourly hire and private events in London, Paris, Bulgaria and Dubai. Service buttons pre-fill the shared quote form. Preserve domain identifiers, service detail URLs, metadata and the existing booking workflow.

English, French, Bulgarian and Arabic are supported through typed dictionaries, including right-to-left Arabic layout. Service descriptions and inclusions are sourced from those dictionaries. Do not invent prices, statistics or commercial claims.

## Brand Commitments

The user explicitly requires the existing theme, colors and typography to remain consistent with the web application. The redesign should feel modern and elite while retaining Driveo Exclusive's established identity.

## Evidence on Hand

Product copy: `frontend/src/i18n/dictionaries/`. Brand tokens: `frontend/src/styles/globals.css`. Existing fleet and destination photography: `frontend/public/media/`. Application conventions and API contract: `frontend/README.md`.
